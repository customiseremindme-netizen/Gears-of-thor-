# GOT FITNEZZ website — owner guide

Your website has two parts:

- **The website** that visitors see: `yourdomain.com`
- **The dashboard** where you make changes: `yourdomain.com/admin`

> **The golden rule:** everything you change in the dashboard is saved as a **draft**. Visitors only see it after you press **Publish**. So you can try things safely, preview them, and publish when you are happy.

## Signing in

1. Go to `yourdomain.com/admin`.
2. Enter your email and password, then press **Sign in**.
3. You are signed out automatically after 4 hours without activity.

Forgotten your password? Another owner can set a new one under **Users**. If nobody can sign in, follow the steps on the **Forgotten your password?** page (you will need Hostinger’s File Manager).

## Your first steps

Open **Owner checklist** in the dashboard menu. It lists what still needs your confirmation, such as opening hours, training options and the privacy notice, with a button to fix each one. Anything not yet confirmed stays hidden from visitors.

## Editing text

1. Open **Page sections**.
2. Press **Edit** next to the section you want to change, for example *Hero* or *Training*.
3. Change the text.
   - In headings, press **Enter** to start a new line.
   - Put `*stars*` around a word to show it in your accent colour. For example `BUILD *YOURSELF.*`
4. Press **Save draft**, or **Save and preview** to see it straight away.
5. When you are happy, press **Publish** (top of the dashboard).

In FAQ answers and the privacy notice you can type these shortcuts. They are replaced with your current details automatically:

- `[phone]` your phone number (as a tap-to-call link)
- `[address]` your address
- `[hours]` your opening hours (or “please call” while they are not confirmed)
- `[training]` your training options
- `[instagram]` your Instagram link

## Photos and the hero video

**Upload photos**

1. Open **Photos & videos**.
2. Drag photos onto the box, or press **Choose files**. You can select several at once.
3. Open a photo and add a short **description** of what it shows (for example “Dumbbell rack and benches at GOT FITNEZZ”). This helps people who use screen readers, and helps Google.

Photos are resized automatically for fast loading, and hidden camera data such as GPS location is removed.

**Use a photo in a section**

In any section, find the photo box and press **Choose from library**, or **Upload new** to add one directly.

**Replace a photo everywhere**

Open the photo in **Photos & videos** and use **Upload replacement**. The new photo takes its place everywhere in your draft. Then preview and publish.

**Good photos to use**

- Your own gym: equipment, training areas, the entrance, people training *with their permission*.
- Wide (landscape) photos at least 1920 pixels across for the hero; any shape works in the gallery.
- Tip: your Instagram already has photos. Save the best originals from your phone and upload those.
- Do not use stock photos of people. Visitors would assume they are your trainers or members.

**Hero video (optional)**

- Open **Page sections → Hero → Background video**.
- Use an MP4 file, 10–20 seconds long, with no sound, ideally under 15 MB.
- Always add a **background photo** too. Phones show the photo instead of the video, to save mobile data.

## The gym gallery

1. Open **Page sections → The Gym**.
2. Under **Photos**, press **Upload several photos** and pick your gym photos.
3. Optionally add a caption to each photo, or set its size (Wide, Tall, Standard).
4. Use the ↑ ↓ arrows to change the order. ✕ removes a photo from the gallery (it stays in the library).
5. Save and publish. The section, its menu link and the “Explore the Gym” button appear once there are photos.

**Facilities** (air conditioning, lockers, parking…) appear only when you switch them on. Only switch on what is really available.

## Opening hours, phone, address and WhatsApp

Open **Business info & hours**.

- **Phone** is used by every Call button.
- **WhatsApp** shows a button only when you add a number **and** switch on “Show the WhatsApp button”.
- **Google Maps link**: in the Google Maps app, find GOT FITNEZZ, tap **Share**, copy the link and paste it here. This gives visitors exact directions.
- **Opening hours**:
  - Enter up to three sessions per day. Leave a day empty if the gym is closed.
  - **Copy Monday to Tuesday–Saturday** saves typing.
  - Switch on **These hours are correct** to show them. Until then, visitors are asked to call.

## Training, membership, coaches, reviews and stories

- **Training**: edit the three options, or switch on extra ones such as personal training *only if you offer them*. Please do not use the word “CrossFit” unless you are an official CrossFit affiliate.
- **Membership**: without plans this is a simple “Ask About Membership” panel. Add plans only with real, current prices. Do not mention offers or free trials unless they are confirmed.
- **Coaches**: add real photos, names and roles. Qualifications appear only after you tick “I have checked these qualifications”.
- **Member reviews**: copy genuine reviews word for word, say where they were posted (for example Google), and tick the member’s permission. Never write reviews yourself.
- **Member stories**: share a transformation only with the member’s written consent, and do not promise results.

Each of these sections stays hidden until it has real content.

## Showing, hiding and reordering sections

Open **Page sections**:

- Drag a section, or use ↑ ↓, to change the order.
- Switch **Show** off to hide a section.
- The Hero always stays at the top, and the Visit & contact section (with the form) is always shown.

Press **Save order**, then preview and publish.

## Managing enquiries

Every callback request appears under **Enquiries**, newest first. The menu shows how many are new.

1. Tap the mobile number to **call**, or use **WhatsApp**.
2. Change the **status** as you follow up:
   - **New**: not contacted yet
   - **Contacted**: you spoke to them
   - **Follow-up**: needs another call
   - **Joined**: became a member
3. Open an enquiry to add **notes**, for example “Visiting Saturday morning”. Notes are never shown on the website.
4. Use the tabs to filter by status, and the search box to find a name or number.
5. **Download CSV** gives you a spreadsheet of the enquiries you are viewing (opens in Excel or Google Sheets).

The **Spam** tab holds enquiries that were filtered automatically. If a real person ended up there, open it and choose **Not spam**. Spam is deleted automatically after 30 days.

If someone asks you to remove their details, open their enquiry and choose **Delete permanently** (owners only).

## Preview and publish

1. Open **Preview & publish** to see your draft. Switch between **Desktop**, **Tablet** and **Phone** views.
2. Sections that are hidden on the live site show as a dashed box, with a hint about what they need.
3. Press **Publish now** to make your changes live, or **Discard changes** to throw the draft away.

Made a mistake? **Publish history** keeps your recent versions. Press **Restore to draft** on an earlier one, check it, then publish.

## Logo and colours

**Logo & colours** lets you replace the logo and the small browser icon, and change the colours. The accent colour is taken from the lime shield in your logo. The page checks the colours as you change them and warns you if text would become hard to read.

## Search and sharing

**Search & sharing** controls the title and description shown in Google, and the picture shown when your link is shared on WhatsApp or Facebook (best size 1200 × 630 pixels). Changes can take a few days to appear in Google.

## People and passwords (owners)

Under **Users** you can give each person their own sign-in:

- **Owner**: everything, including users and settings
- **Editor**: website content, photos, publishing and enquiries
- **Staff**: enquiries only

Never share one password between people. Change your own password under **My account**.

## Email alerts and spam protection (owners)

Under **Settings** you can:

- **Get an email for every new enquiry.** Press **Send a test email** to check it works, and look in the spam folder the first time.
- **Add Cloudflare Turnstile** (free) if spam ever becomes a problem. The built-in spam filters are already switched on.
- **Update the website address** when you move to your final domain.

## Backups

Hostinger makes automatic backups (hPanel → **Files → Backups**). Before big changes, you can also download:

- the **database** (hPanel → Databases → phpMyAdmin → Export), and
- the **public_html/public/uploads** folder (your photos).

## If something looks wrong

- Not seeing your change? Check you pressed **Publish**, then refresh the page.
- Photo will not upload? Use JPG or PNG under 20 MB. iPhone HEIC photos and MOV videos need converting first (share or export them as JPG / MP4).
- Still stuck? Any web developer can read `docs/DEPLOY-HOSTINGER.md` and the error log in `storage/logs`.
